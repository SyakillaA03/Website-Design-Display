About ZEOCHEM

ZEOCHEM, a company of CPH Chemie & Papier Holding AG, is a global Swiss-based manufacturer specializing in high-performance molecular sieves and adsorbents tailored for the gas, LNG, petrochemical, and industrial sectors. With decades of manufacturing expertise, ZEOCHEM delivers reliable solutions that satisfy demanding process requirements worldwide.
