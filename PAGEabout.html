About Us

TNZ Engineering Services Sdn. Bhd. has a rich history that began with its establishment on May 21, 2009, and its official commencement of operations in March 2012. The company's journey traces back to its predecessor, TNZ Services Sdn Bhd, which initially started in 1997 as a trading entity specializing in instrumentation piping parts. Evolving with the industry's demands, TNZ Services Sdn Bhd transitioned into installing complete gas piping systems, diversifying its service portfolio.

Today, TNZ Engineering Services Sdn. Bhd. stands as a reputable and dependable player in the field, boasting an impressive clientele that includes esteemed organizations such as Petronas, SIRIM, MARDI, MINDEF, STRIDE, Agensi Nuklear Malaysia, UTP, UITM, UKM, UPM, UTM, UNISEL, among others. The company has also established itself as a preferred contractor for renowned analytical equipment suppliers like Perkin Elmer, Agilent Technologies, and Lab Alliance.

While gas piping remains at the core of its business activities, TNZ Engineering Services Sdn. Bhd. has expanded its scope to encompass engineering maintenance works tailored to its clients' unique requirements. Additionally, the company offers general trading services as a supplementary component of its comprehensive service offerings. With a commitment to quality, reliability, and a diverse range of services, TNZ Engineering Services Sdn. Bhd. continues to be a trusted partner in the industry.

We Supply

Laboratory Consumables

Laboratory consumables related to gas piping supply are essential items used to ensure safety, maintain cleanliness, and facilitate experiments or processes involving gases in a laboratory setting.

Laboratory Safety Devices

Laboratory safety devices related to gas supply systems, particularly gas detection systems, are critical for monitoring and detecting hazardous gases, leaks, or abnormal conditions to protect personnel.

Special Gas Related Parts

Specialized gas-related parts, such as regulators, valves, and fittings, are essential for the safe and precise control of gas flow, pressure, and distribution in laboratory and industrial settings.

General Laboratory Equipments

A comprehensive range of instruments and devices used to control, monitor, and handle gases effectively, maintaining a controlled and secure environment during lab operations.

Hardware & Machineries

Essential machinery components, cutting tools, drills, and installation accessories required for assembling, modifying, and maintaining robust gas supply piping systems.
